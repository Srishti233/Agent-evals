# Product: Atlas
Atlas is the flagship autonomous mobile robot from Lumora Labs and it is designed to move pallets inside warehouses. Atlas was launched in March 2019. It can carry a payload of 1,200 kg and travels at a top speed of 2.0 m/s. A fully charged battery lasts 8 hours and a full charge takes 90 minutes. The list price is $24,000 per unit.
